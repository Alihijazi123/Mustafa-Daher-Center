Drilling and Equipping Water Wells

The drilling and equipping of water wells we undertake follows a sequence of work phases that ensures proper completion:

  1. Site Assessment: Upon receiving a customer request, we check our Hydro-geological Information System, conduct a field survey, check the related legal aspects, and then we apply for a formal approval from the concerned ministry.
  2. Locating Underground Water: Examining the site and the hydro geological maps, then using specialized tools to locate the underground basin of usable water.
  3. Rig Setup: Cleaning the workspace, leveling the drilling machine (rotary or hammer), and setting up mud and drainage sewers and pits.
  4. Hole Drilling: Initiating the drilling works until water is found.
  5. Well Casing: Inserting metal pipes, or PVC / stainless steel / other, depending on client request and relevant conditions, into the hole to let the water in and blocking loose dirt and sand from caving in. This phase takes place simultaneously with the drilling works.
  6. Grouting: It consists of sealing the space outside the pipe with clay or cement, depending on the site geological structure.
  7. Pump Setup: Selecting the right submersible or surface pump depends on the tests we make to determine the water volume / depth level and the pump output volume requested by customer.
  8. Wellhead Cap Setup: Installing sanitary seals made of concrete aprons and secure caps at the top surface of the well to block dirt, insects, and runoff contamination.
  9. Power Systems Setup: Installing grid, diesel generator, and/or solar panels to run the pump.
  10. Control and Automation Setup: Installing water level sensors, float switches, digital timing controls, pressure switches, and dry-run protection / safety sensors.
  11. Water Filters Setup: Installing, if need be, filters for cleaning the water of the well.
  12. Final Testing: Making final tests to check the mechanical and electrical systems to validate adherence to the technical standards and ensuring customer satisfaction.
