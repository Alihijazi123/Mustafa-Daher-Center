What We Do

  • Drilling and equipping water wells, installing solar power systems for pumps, and providing the corresponding general maintenance.
  • Conducting hydrogeological research and studies.
  • Selling submersible and surface pumps, inverters, control panels, AC output reactors, electric cables, pipes, and accessories.

Birth and Evolution

Moustapha Daher Center was founded in 1998 by Moustapha Daher, a resilient entrepreneur who started his career as a field technician. He gradually acquired and compiled a solid work experience that turned him into one of the most renowned innovators and experts of water technologies.

Since established, Moustapha Daher Center has been steadily expanding its expertise, increasing its market share, and enhancing its well-earned reputation.
